Data contributor

This is LADA’s interface for data contributors, the people who publish research data.

LADA (License Aware Digital Assistant) helps social science researchers share open data and reuse it in line with its licenses. This page covers the first of those two cases. The contributor answers questions about the rights they hold and about what they want to allow or restrict for the people who will use the data: access, purposes of reuse, reproduction, distribution, derivative works, attribution, and the other obligations attached to publication.

From those answers, the system proposes a license for the data. The model is the Creative Commons license chooser, extended to the licenses used for social science data: Creative Commons, Open Data Commons, and public-sector open-data licenses. Those licenses are expressed in ODRL 2.2, a machine-readable format, so that LADA can then determine which uses are permitted.
